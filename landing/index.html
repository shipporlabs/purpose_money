How it works

From a wallet abroad to a receipt from the shop down the road.

STEP 1

Siblings fund

Each child sends from any chain. NEAR Intents converts it to a stablecoin.

STEP 2

Split by purpose

Food, rent, school fees, or any purpose the family names.

STEP 3

Guardian checks

Parents request a payment. The guardian approves it if it fits, or asks the siblings.

STEP 4

Shop paid in naira

It lands in the shop's bank account as a normal transfer.

STEP 5

Everyone sees it

Every sibling gets the same receipt. Unspent money rolls over.

The family pot

One pot. Every sibling. Decisions made together.

Each pot is a shared wallet on NEAR, run on Sputnik DAO. Siblings are its members, so the money and the rules belong to the whole family, not one person.

The AI guardian

It can approve a payment. It can't take one.

The guardian checks every request against the family's rules. Its key has one job: approving payments that fit. It can't change rules, add members or move money on its own.

  • Checks the rules: approved shop, category budget, daily cap, cooling-off for new shops, account name match.
  • Escalates the unusual: anything outside the rules goes to the siblings.
  • Parents can only ask: a stolen phone can't send money anywhere.

Questions, answered

Do my parents need to understand crypto?

No. They see balances in naira, scan or tap to pay, and never pay transaction fees.

Who controls the family pot?

The siblings, together. Big changes like new budgets or members need the votes the family agreed on.

What exactly can the AI guardian do?

Only approve payment requests that follow the family's rules, and flag the rest. It can't move money itself, and the siblings can remove it at any time.

How do shops get paid?

By normal naira bank transfer. Any shop, school or landlord with a Nigerian bank account can be paid.

What if a parent's phone is stolen?

The phone's key can only request payments to approved shops. Anything else is blocked and the siblings are alerted.

What happens to money that isn't spent?

It stays in the pot and rolls over to next month, unless the family votes to return it.
